🐑 About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Technology at Sichuan University, advised by Prof. Yi Zhang (张意) (Deep Imaging Group). Since 2025, I have been a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy, supervised by Dr. Weihua Chen (陈威华) and Dr. Zhihui Lin (林智慧).


My research interests lie in computer vision, image generation, and inverse problems in medical imaging, with a particular focus on CT image reconstruction and metal artifact reduction.


My research codes are open-sourced on GitHub. If you find my work useful, please consider starring the repositories and citing my papers.
