01About简介

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Sichuan University, advised by Prof. Yi Zhang (Deep Imaging Group). Since 2025, I have been a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy with Dr. Weihua Chen and Dr. Zhihui Lin.

My research focuses on computer vision, image generation, and inverse problems in medical imaging — particularly CT reconstruction and metal artifact reduction.
